Home-Based Technical Training Plan

5–6 Days / Week  ·  15–20 Min Per Session  ·  Ball Only

How this plan works: Each week has Video-Guided days and Self-Directed days. On Video-Guided days Benjamin watches a Ball Mastery Video Capsule (tap the button to open it), then practices around what he watched. On Self-Directed days he works independently on a set focus. Sunday is always rest.
Capsule rotation
🔥 Warm-Up With the Ball 3–4 min
  • Dribble in small circles, change direction every 5 touches.
  • Alternate feet — left, right, left, right — keep it smooth.
  • Add a few toe taps or step-overs just to wake up coordination.
  • Goal: get a feel for the ball, loosen up — no rushing.
⚽ Core Technical Focus 8–10 min
📹 Video-Guided Days
  • Open today's video from the Weekly Plan tab — watch it fully first.
  • Immediately replicate the movements shown — do 2–3 sets of the key drill.
  • Focus on one thing at a time: shape, touch, or rhythm.
  • Rest 20–30 seconds between sets — quality over speed.
👆 Video links are on the Weekly Plan tab — tap "Watch Video" next to today's day.
🏃 Self-Directed Days
  • Pick today's focus drill (first touch, coordination, or wall work — see weekly plan).
  • Do 3 rounds of the drill, resting 20–30 seconds between each.
  • Switch feet or directions halfway through each round.
  • Ask yourself: 'Is this touch clean? Is my body in the right position?'
✓ What does "clean" mean?
›
Ball Mastery & Dribbling
Clean = you can pause mid-move and hold the ball under your foot without adjusting. No double taps, no chasing the ball.
First Touch & Receiving
Clean = ball stays on the ground, your first touch moves it 1–2 feet away from your body, and you don't need to chase it or take an extra touch to control it.
Wall Passing
Clean = ball returns to you within 1 meter, you receive it with one touch, and you're ready to pass again without adjusting your position.
Coordination & Footwork
Clean = you hit the rhythm without stumbling, both feet are working, and you can complete the pattern three times in a row without stopping.

💡 If you're rushing and losing control, cut your target in half and slow down. Quality always beats speed.

🎯 Short Challenge or Constraint 3–4 min
  • Apply what you just practiced in a mini-challenge (e.g., '10 clean first touches in a row without losing the ball').
  • Set a small, honest goal — it should feel achievable but not easy.
  • If you hit it, try to beat it. If not, that's your target for next time.
  • End on a positive rep — finish with a clean, confident touch.
🌙 Cool-Down 1–2 min
  • Dribble slowly, no pressure — just enjoy the ball at your feet.
  • Think: 'What was one thing I did well today?'
  • Stretch legs gently for 30 seconds each side.
⏱️ Total Session: 15–20 minutes
⚠️ What if I miss my target?
Progress isn't linear — bad days happen. If you miss your success criterion 2 sessions in a row, drop back to the previous week's target. No shame, no stress — that's smart training. Ask yourself: "What was different? Sleep? Surface? Distraction?" This helps you learn what affects your performance without blaming yourself.
